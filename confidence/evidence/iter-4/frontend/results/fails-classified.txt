## 1. v4-reconnect: "after the restart the badge returns to Live by itself within the 10 s cap (plus one attempt)"
#  measured 13.9 s, bound 11 s. CLASS: script assumption made obsolete by the deliberate G-4-17 change, not a product failure.
#  The bound dates from iteration 2, when the badge read Live on the stream's open event. Since G-4-17 (commit 27a9fec) a
#  stream counts as working only after a frame or 6 s open (frontend/README.md:39-46, STREAM_SETTLE_MS in api.ts), and a
#  resume with nothing missed carries no frame. Decomposition from results/v4-reconnect-timeline.txt:
#    +59.0s stream REQUEST #11 ?lastEventId=245  -> 502   (backend A still starting)
#    +61.3s A up again: true                              (t_up)
#    +69.0s stream REQUEST #12 ?lastEventId=245  -> 200   7.7 s after t_up: inside the 10 s cap (previous attempt +59.0s)
#    +75.1s badge -> Live                                 6.1 s after that 200: the settle time; no frame, nothing was missed
#  13.9 s = 7.7 s (next attempt, <= cap 10 s) + 6.1 s (settle 6 s). Expected upper bound since G-4-17: 10 s + 6 s + slack.
#  The next check of the same run passed: the attempt was a resume (?lastEventId=245) and delivered no snapshot / no replay.
#  The same deliberate change was applied to v7's G-3-20 check BEFORE its run (badge at +5 s now 'Service degraded').
