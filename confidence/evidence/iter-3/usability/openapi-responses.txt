externalRef (CreateCommunicationCommand): Permanent idempotency key, usable once per tenant for the life of the store (retired and expired notices included). Repeat it only to retry the same publish: the 409 names the notice that already holds it. A follow-up notice needs a new value, for example INC0012345-2. Defaults to ticket.number when a ticket is given.
expectedVersion (UpdateCommunicationCommand): The version you last read: every answer and GET /v1/communications/{id} carry it. A 409 'expectedVersion N does not match current version M' means someone saved in between: re-read, re-apply your change and send the new version.
