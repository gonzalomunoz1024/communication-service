Fix G-1-3: fall back to a fresh snapshot when a resume never opens

The reopen added for G-1-3 passes ?lastEventId=<last seq> so the service can
replay what was missed. If that request is refused (today's service answers a
resume with 500, G-1-1, and a proxy answers 502 while it restarts) the browser
closes the EventSource again and the client would retry the same resume for
ever, never getting back on the air.

api.ts: when a resumed connection closes without ever having opened, the next
attempt drops lastEventId and starts from a snapshot; a resumed connection
that did open keeps resuming from the newest seq. useOutageFeed already
reloads the list once the stream is back, so nothing is lost either way.

Tests (src/test/stream.test.ts): a failed resume is followed by a plain
reopen, frames seen on a later connection are resumed again, and a resume
that opens keeps resuming. 12 files / 78 tests pass.
