Fix G-1-12: move focus to the first invalid field after Publish

Publishing an incomplete form rendered the errors next to their fields, far
above the button, and left focus on the button, so on a long page nothing
visibly happened (UX-1).

- PublishOutageForm: validation faults are ordered as the fields appear;
  the errors are flushed synchronously and focus (plus the viewport, centred)
  moves to the first faulted field, for Severity to its selected segment, and
  the Timing card opens first when the fault is in there. An error summary
  ("3 fields need attention: Title (...), ...") with links that focus each
  field renders above the Publish button, where the user is looking. Every
  input now points at its hint/error line with aria-describedby and the
  datetime inputs carry aria-invalid.
- Field: `noteId` (defaults to `${htmlFor}-note`) gives the hint/error line
  an id for aria-describedby.
- Segmented: `id`, `describedBy` and `invalid` props.

Tests (src/test/PublishOutageForm.test.tsx, jsdom + user-event): empty form ->
Title focused, aria-invalid, accessible description, summary text, summary
link focuses Publisher and the summary shrinks as fields are fixed; maintenance
without an end -> Timing opens and Ends is focused; Info outage -> the Severity
group is focused and marked invalid. Against the previous form all 3 fail; with
the fix 10 files / 58 tests pass.
