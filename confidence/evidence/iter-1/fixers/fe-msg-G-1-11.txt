Fix G-1-11: filter stream frames by the feed's visibility rule

feed-state.ts kept every SCHEDULED frame, while GET /v1/feed lists only what is
live plus scheduled MAINTENANCE starting within communication.feed.upcoming-window
(P7D). A scheduled outage therefore appeared under "Upcoming" as long as the
stream delivered it and vanished on the next reload.

- feed-state.ts: inFeed() mirrors CommunicationQuery.visibleAt (ACTIVE, or
  SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE with startsAt <= now + UPCOMING_WINDOW_MS); applyEvent()
  upserts only items that pass it and drops an item an update moves out.
  UPCOMING_WINDOW_MS is documented as the counterpart of the service setting.
- README: states the rule once for both paths.

Tests (src/test/feed-state.test.ts): maintenance 3 days out is listed, 10 days
out is not; scheduled outage/incident/change are not listed until they start and
an update that pushes a live item into the future removes it; inFeed at the
window boundary. Against the previous reducer 3 of the new tests fail; with the
fix 9 files / 55 tests pass.
