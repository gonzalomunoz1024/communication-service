Fix G-1-16: time-driven card states, page cap notice, Withdraw, 409 wording

Four small feed/form gaps (GAP-frontend-7/8, UX-8, UX-9):

- A card lingered after expiresAt (still offering Resolve) until the sweeper
  frame arrived, and an upcoming maintenance did not turn active at startsAt.
  feed-state.ts gains revise() (re-projects statuses at an instant and drops
  what left the feed) and nextChange() (the next start/expiry boundary);
  useOutageFeed wakes up at that boundary and projects again, so cards move on
  time without a frame.
- The 100-item page cap was silent (hasNext ignored). The hook exposes
  total/truncated from the page and the feed says "Showing the first N of M
  notices. Narrow the filters to see the rest."
- "Resolve" on a notice that has not started now reads "Withdraw", with
  matching confirmation copy and announcement; both retire it.
- The service's 409 "duplicate externalRef '...' for tenant '...'" is put in
  the form's terms by explainPublishError(): the field people filled in is
  the ServiceNow incident number, which doubles as the reference.
- The empty state says what the feed lists (live notices; maintenance planned
  within the next 7 days; other scheduled notices once they start) instead of
  "active or upcoming".
- README documents the behaviour.

Tests: feed-state.test.ts (revise/nextChange across boundaries, stable array
when idle), useOutageFeed.test.tsx (expiry drops a card and a start activates
one with real timers and no frame; truncated/total), OutageFeed.test.tsx (cap
notice, empty-state copy), OutageCard.test.tsx (Withdraw flow),
errors.test.ts and PublishOutageForm.test.tsx (409 wording). Against the
previous sources 9 of the new tests fail; with the fix 12 files / 77 tests pass.
