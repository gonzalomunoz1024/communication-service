Fix G-1-13: "View in feed" lands on the feed that shows the new notice

After publishing, "View in feed" switched tabs but kept the header tenant and
the default Type=Outage filter, so an incident, maintenance or change, or a
notice published under another tenant, was nowhere to be seen (UX-2, UX-3).

- PublishOutageForm: onViewFeed receives the published communication.
- App: on "View in feed" the header tenant becomes the notice's tenant and the
  feed opens with its type filter set to the notice's type (OutageFeed's
  defaultType is now driven by the shell).
- README: documents the callback.

Test (src/test/App.test.tsx, jsdom, stubbed fetch/EventSource): publish a
CHANGE for tenant "other" from the shell, click "View in feed", and the header
reads "other", the Type filter reads Change, the feed GET carries
tenantId=other&type=CHANGE and the new notice is on screen. Against the
previous shell it fails; with the fix 11 files / 59 tests pass.
