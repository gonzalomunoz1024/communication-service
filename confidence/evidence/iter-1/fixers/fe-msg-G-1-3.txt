Fix G-1-3: reopen the live stream after the browser gives up

EventSource retries a dropped socket on its own, but stops for good once a
retry is answered with anything other than 200 (service restart, proxy 502).
The feed then showed "Reconnecting" forever while nothing reconnected.

- api.ts: when the browser has given up (readyState CLOSED) the client closes
  the source and reopens it with exponential backoff (1 s, 2 s, ... 30 s),
  passing ?lastEventId=<highest seq seen> so the service replays the gap; a
  successful open resets the backoff; unsubscribe cancels a pending reopen.
  New optional onRetry(attempt, delayMs) handler, exported reconnectDelay().
- useOutageFeed.ts: rewritten around derived state (no setState in effects);
  exposes reconnectAttempt, reloads the page once after a recovery and keeps
  stream updates received meanwhile (feed-state reconcile()); refresh()
  reopens the stream immediately while it is down.
- OutageFeed.tsx: the badge shows "Reconnecting (n)".
- vite.config.ts: the dev proxy destroys the browser response when the
  upstream SSE socket closes, so the browser notices a backend restart
  instead of hanging on a half-open socket (http-proxy never ends it).
- README: documents the reconnect behaviour.

A watchdog on the 15 s keepalive comments is not possible from the page:
EventSource does not surface comment lines, so a silently dead socket can
only be detected at the proxy/TCP layer, which the proxy change covers in
development.

Tests: src/test/stream.test.ts (fake EventSource + fake timers: CLOSED ->
reopen with backoff and lastEventId, CONNECTING -> no reopen, unsubscribe
cancels), src/test/useOutageFeed.test.tsx (reconnecting/attempt/live, reload
after recovery, refresh reopens), src/test/OutageFeed.test.tsx (badge
"Reconnecting (3)"), src/test/feed-state.test.ts (reconcile). Against the
previous sources 8 of the new tests fail; with the fix 7 files / 38 tests pass.
