Fix G-1-15: announce outcomes, keep focus, name every control

Accessibility gaps from the usability pass (UX-7, UX-14) and the frontend
verifier (GAP-4): nothing was announced after Resolve, focus fell to <body>
after publishing, the feed had no live region, the "Service" filter had no
accessible name, the page had no h1 and the Type/Severity labels were not tied
to their groups.

- OutageFeed: a polite role=status line announces (and shows for 8 s)
  "Resolved <title>. It has left every consumer's feed."; the list wrapper
  takes focus when the resolved card disappears; Service/Type/Minimum severity
  filters are labelled through htmlFor/id.
- PublishOutageForm: the Type and Severity groups are named by their visible
  labels (Field labelId + Segmented ariaLabelledBy); the three cards' titles
  are h2; the confirmation heading is an h2 that receives focus when the
  outcome replaces the form.
- App: the title is an h1 (visually hidden on phones, where the header has no
  room for it).
- Kit: Field `labelId`, Segmented `ariaLabelledBy` (ariaLabel now optional),
  Card `titleAs`.

Tests: OutageFeed.test.tsx (filters resolve through getByLabelText, status
text after a resolve, focus stays on the list), PublishOutageForm.test.tsx
(radiogroups named Type/Severity, heading outline h2 only, focus on "Outage
published"), App.test.tsx (h1). Against the previous sources 4 of the new
assertions fail; with the fix 12 files / 68 tests pass.
