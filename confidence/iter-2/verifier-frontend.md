## 5. Not exercised in this sandbox

- A real OpenShift Route / ingress in front of the UI and the `restricted-v2` SCC admission itself: the pod was emulated with Docker flags (`--user 1000660000:0 --read-only --cap-drop ALL --security-opt no-new-privileges --network container:`), which is what the sandbox allows; the gate belongs to the Sidecar Verifier.
- Retention expiry by time: `communication.events.retention` is one day and the shared log is five hours old, so the expired-id fallback was proven by deleting old events from my private database (`v7b`), which is what the TTL index does later; no 24 h wait is possible here.
- Browsers other than Chromium: Playwright's Firefox and WebKit are not installed and `npx playwright install` is out of bounds; screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver) are not available, so accessibility rests on axe, DOM roles and names, keyboard behaviour and contrast sampling.
- `npm run build` itself was not run because its `dist/` output is served by the E2E Verifier's `vite preview`; its two halves (`tsc -b`, `vite build`) were run separately to a scratch folder with identical bundle hashes.
- The exit codes of the five browser runs marked (a) in section 2 were not captured; their complete per-check verdicts are in the logs and results.
- A real ServiceNow instance: only the composition of `ticket.url` from `communication.servicenow.instance-url` was checked (backend B), not the link target.
- Clock skew between browser and service, and locales other than the emulated Europe/Madrid, were not varied.
