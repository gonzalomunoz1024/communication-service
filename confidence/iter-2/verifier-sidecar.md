Observations (not sidecar defects; recorded for the auditor and the other verifiers):

- **O-1 (backend, F-92/G-2-4):** with Mongo *paused* (TCP black hole, not connection refused) the service needs 20.1 s to
  answer 503 `database unavailable` (`ev/16` CASE 3), not the 5 s server-selection timeout G-2-4 describes; the sidecar
  is unaffected (its own 5 s `CS_REPORT_TIMEOUT` fires first and it retries). For the Backend Verifier.
- **O-2 (F-74):** the heartbeat is evaluated on the probe tick, so the cadence is `CS_HEARTBEAT` rounded up to the next
  jittered tick: 3 heartbeats in 24 s at 6 s/2 s (`ev/11` STEP 6), 32.4 s at 30 s/3 s (`ev/12` PHASE 1); README L108
  says "every 30 s". Unchanged from iteration 1.
- **O-3 (F-83):** the strategic-merge component inserts `health-sidecar` *before* the app's own init containers
  (`ev/20` §2); now documented in `add-native-sidecar.yaml:3-4`. Helm consumers choose the position (`ev/20` §3).
- **O-4 (F-48):** the silence rule was verified with the staleness valve disabled on my private backend
  (`stale-pause-min-silent=1000`), because a database holding only short-lived test monitors would otherwise pause it
  (G-2-5 semantics, the Backend Verifier's F-45). With that, every SIGKILLed pod became `DOWN (1 pod has not reported
  since …)` + OUTAGE ≈ 92 s after its last report, and the SIGTERM'd ones were scaled to zero.
- **O-5 (F-38/F-70):** the sidecar's own vocabulary is wider than the service's (`PASS/GREEN/SERVING → UP`,
  `FAIL/ERROR/RED/NOT_SERVING → DOWN` are normalised before sending, `probe.go` `Normalize`); a raw `PASS` posted by
  another publisher is `DEGRADED` on the service (`ev/15`), which is F-38's documented "anything else" rule.
- **O-6 (hygiene):** unlike iteration 1 (O-4 there), no scenario touched the shared backend; the shared pod and backend
  were only read. The `vs2_*` databases on the shared Mongo were left in place (test data under my prefix).
