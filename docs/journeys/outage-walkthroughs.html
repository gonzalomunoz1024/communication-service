Communication service, step by step

Every way the outage service gets used, shown as it happened: the screens people see, the answers systems get, and what the health sidecar and the service wrote while it ran.

Captured 2026-11-02 13:04 UTC by driving the real stack: the React screens at 1280×800 in headless Chromium, the API over HTTP with the X-Client-Id header, the Go health sidecar in its container next to a stand-in app, and the service's own log. Nothing here is mocked; the short timers (probe every 5 s, a 75 s maintenance window) are walkthrough settings, the defaults are stated in each step. Code at commit 1cd3f13.
